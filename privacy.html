Privacy

FieldView is a static website. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no server of its own that stores anything about you.

Analytics. The site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymous usage statistics, such as which pages are visited. It does not ask for your name or email.

Your browser. Preferences (for example the sidebar On/Off toggle, substitution sort order, a selected formation, a pasted mock draft, or a dismissed notice) are saved only in your own browser's local storage. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Nothing else is collected.
